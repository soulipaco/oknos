# Licence for open content packs

Everything under `content/open/` is licensed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)**,
unless a pack's own `pack.json` says otherwise.

- Human-readable summary: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Full legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

Attribution: "Oknos contributors", with a link to this repository.

Every pack records its licence in the `licence` field of its `pack.json`, and a test
checks that each pack here is marked `"visibility": "open"` with an open licence.

The code of the app is under the MIT licence in the top-level `LICENSE` file.
Nothing copied from textbooks, publisher audio or past exam papers may be placed
here; that material belongs in `content/private/`, which is never tracked.
