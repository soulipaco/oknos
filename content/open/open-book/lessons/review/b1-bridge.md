# Review: b1-bridge

Drafted by Sonnet (claude-sonnet), reviewed by Opus on 2026-11-20.

## Corrected
- The αν section and a tip said flatly that θα never follows αν. True in a condition, but the same chapter teaches «ρώτησε αν …», where αν means "whether" and θα is normal («Με ρώτησε αν θα έρθω»). Both places now say "in a condition", and the tip shows the reported-question case.
- Greek word order in the library letter: «Είναι η βιβλιοθήκη ανοιχτή το απόγευμα;» → «Είναι ανοιχτή η βιβλιοθήκη το απόγευμα;».
- Turkish: Καλαμαριά was rendered "Kalamaris'te" (the book's Turkish name is Kalamarya); Turkish suffixes glued to αν / θα rephrased.

## Unsure
- «Με λένε Ζεϊνέπ» in a formal letter is plain; «Ονομάζομαι …» is the formal register. Kept at the learner's level; worth teaching later.
- The 130-170 word range in the exam tip was checked against exams/b1-keg.json (b1keg.writing.formal).
- The validator notes «δήμο» and «καλή» as untaught: forms of ο δήμος (this chapter's word, a four-letter stem the stem check cannot match) and of καλός (καλά is known). Both have meanings when tapped.

## Validate
No problems. The passive forms are mapped to their verbs; Αξιότιμε, Με εκτίμηση and επισκευάστηκε got glossary rows.
