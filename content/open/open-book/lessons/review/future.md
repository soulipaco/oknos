# Review: future

Drafted by Sonnet (claude-sonnet), reviewed by Opus on 2026-11-20.

## Corrected
- Greek: «στο φούρνο» three times (dialogue, example, a check); with a masculine noun it is «στον φούρνο», the rule the shopping and getting-around chapters teach.
- «Θα δω τον γιατρό τη Δευτέρα» was translated «Pazartesi doktora gideceğim»; now «Pazartesi doktorla görüşeceğim», closer to the Greek.

## Unsure
- Two distractors are invented over-regularisations (ταξιδέσει, θα βλέπσω). They are not real forms the learner might have met, so they cannot mislead; kept.
- The 1pl stress note (θα δούμε, θα πούμε … but θα πάμε, θα φάμε) lists the verbs by name rather than stating a rule; correct as written.
- Every future form in the tables was checked against open-verbs-a1a2 or standard grammar (θα πάμε, θα φάμε, θα φας, θα δούμε, θα πούμε, θα βρούμε, θα μπούμε, θα βγούμε are not in the pack and are correct).

## Validate
No problems. Lone stem endings in the pattern table (-σω, -ψω, -ήσω …) have glossary rows explaining them.
