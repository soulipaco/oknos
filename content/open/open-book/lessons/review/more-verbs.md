# Review: more-verbs

Drafted by Sonnet (claude-sonnet), reviewed by Opus on 2026-11-20.

## Corrected
- Greek: Marko answered «Πώς σε λένε;» with «Μάρκο! Κι εσύ;». After a name the natural «and yours?» is «Εσένα;» (it goes with με λένε). Fixed, and glossed in the note after the dialogue.
- The -άω section said the only difference from the -ω verbs was the stress. The endings differ too (μιλάς, not μιλάεις); rewritten to show the endings.
- The -μαι section said these verbs "go like είμαι" and "there is nothing new": not true for the 3rd persons (είναι but έρχεται, έρχονται). Rewritten: the endings recall είμαι but the he/they forms are -ται/-νται.
- The section and the "mistake" tip said the stress moves right in θυμόμαστε and κοιμόμαστε. It does not; there the α becomes ο (θυμάμαι → θυμόμαστε). Both places corrected; the stress shift is kept for έρχομαι and χρειάζομαι.

## Unsure
- The teacher uses εσείς with a first name («Από πού έρχεστε, Μάρκο;»). Normal in adult classes; kept.
- Forms built by pattern and not in the open verb pack were all checked: έρχεσαι, χρειάζεσαι, χρειαζόμαστε, θυμόμαστε, θυμούνται, κοιμάστε, κοιμόμαστε, κοιμούνται, μιλάνε, ρωτάνε, πουλάνε. All correct.
