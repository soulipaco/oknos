## Corrected
- Greek word order: «Ένας μήνας πέρασε στη Θεσσαλονίκη!» is now «Πέρασε ένας μήνας στη Θεσσαλονίκη!» (in the e-mail and in its 50-word version); the verb-first order is what a Greek writes.
- «δεν έχω εκδρομές» → «δεν κάνω εκδρομές» (κάνω εκδρομή is the collocation).
- The tense table glossed ήμουν «idim; yorgundum»; now «idim».
- One Turkish suffix glued to a Greek word (θα'dan) rephrased.

## Unsure

- The e-mail glosses μου αρέσει as met "in the last chapter"; a2-life does teach it (checked after a2-life was final).
- The 45–55 word target in the exam tip is advice within the official 40–60 range.
