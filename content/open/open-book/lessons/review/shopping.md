# Review: shopping

Drafted by Sonnet (claude-sonnet), reviewed by Opus on 2026-11-20.

## Corrected
- Greek: at the end of the market, Zeynep asked the total with a bare «Πόσο κάνουν;». The natural question is «Πόσο κάνουν όλα;». Fixed, and όλα glossed.
- The accusative section said "only masculine nouns and feminine articles change", which leaves out the masculine article; now "masculine: article and ending; feminine: article only; neuter: nothing".
- The plural section said feminine subject and object are the same, then gave a different object article (τις). Rewritten: in the neuter all is the same; in the feminine only the article changes (οι → τις); in the masculine the article and the -ος ending change (τους, -ους).
- «ψωμί → ψωμιά: the stress moves to the end» was wrong (it is on the last syllable in both); now "ια is one syllable, pso-MYA".
- Two form checks used genitive forms the learner has never seen as distractors (μήλων, τυρόπιτας); removed.
- The bakery notice check had «ανοιχτός ως τις τρεις» for 15:00, which needs the afternoon-hour reading taught only in getting-around; the statement is now «Ο φούρνος είναι ανοιχτός το Σάββατο.».
- A comprehension explanation quoted a line the dialogue does not have («Ένα ψωμί, παρακαλώ»); now quotes «Θα ήθελα ένα ψωμί».
- One Turkish suffix glued to a Greek letter (σ'den) rephrased.

## Unsure
- Reading time is about 13 minutes, above the 8-12 target for A1 (inside the validator's 6-16). The chapter carries two grammar points, the days and the numbers to 100, as the outline asks. Left as is; if it feels long in the app, the numbers table could move to getting-around.
- «Κυριακή: κλειστά» (neuter plural, "we are closed") is what shop doors usually say; explained in a tip.
- έναν before a continuant consonant is avoided (only έναν καφέ).

## Validate
No problems. The numbers 11-100, δυόμισι and the -δες plurals have glossary rows or form mappings.
