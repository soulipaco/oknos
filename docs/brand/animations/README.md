# The Rope of Oknos: three films

Three short painted films (about 45 seconds each) for the app's name. Each is a motion comic: 9–10 illustrations with a slow camera move, cross-fades and captions in simple modern Greek, with a Turkish or English line under them (TR/EN switch), so they also work as reading practice.

| File | Story |
|---|---|
| `1-oknos-and-the-donkey.html` | Pausanias 10.29.1: at Delphi, Polygnotus' painting of the underworld shows Oknos plaiting a rope while a she-donkey eats it as fast as he plaits. Day and night, centuries. Then the brand's turn: the donkey stops and carries the coiled rope, and Oknos's new rope grows. Ends on the logo. |
| `2-oknos-and-his-wife.html` | Pausanias 10.29.2: a hard-working Oknos sells rope and fills a jar with coins; his wife spends them as fast; the painter puts a donkey in her place, and she is not amused. |
| `3-the-dream.html` | New: Oknos dozes under an olive tree and dreams the endless story, faster and faster, until the dream donkey reaches his hands. He wakes with a start; the real donkey is only eating a carrot. |

- **The pictures** (`frames/`, 1200×800 WebP) were drawn by an image model through Codex from written storyboards, with the cast's reference sheets attached so the people stay the same (`docs/brand/characters/`: Oknos, the donkey, the wife), and reviewed one by one; 8 of 28 were drawn again (a halter that made the donkey look led, a rope that changed colour, the donkey eating grass instead of rope). The requests and prompts are in `.cache/oknos/pictures/film/` (not tracked).
- **The player** is `film.js` (shots, camera, fades, flash and shake, captions, pause on tap, replay, reduced motion); `anim.css` is the page style. No libraries and no build: serve the folder (`uv run python -m http.server 8670 --directory docs/brand/animations`) or open a file in a browser.
- For checking one moment: `?t=12` starts at second 12, `?still=12` holds the picture there.
- **The voice** (phase 10, `docs/prompts/10-voice.md`): one clip per shot from its Greek caption, named after the shot's picture (`f1-01.mp3`; the end card `f1-end.mp3`). A narrator reads; where Oknos speaks ("Ωχ!" in film 3) a shot's `say` gives the parts and their voices (`m` is Oknos's). **▶ Play with sound** starts the film again with the clips: a shot lasts at least its clip plus a short pause, and the captions stay as they are. Without clips the film plays silently, as before.
- **Two sets of clips**, as decided on 2026-11-22 (Google for the repo, Microsoft's Athina and Nestoras for the maintainer's own use):
  - `voice/`: Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, Chirp 3 HD Kore (narrator) and Orus (Oknos). Tracked and published: Google lets the audio files be used "in your applications or media" under its Cloud terms, on any tier.
  - `voice-local/`: Azure Speech, Athina and Nestoras, not tracked (`.gitignore`): Microsoft's Product Terms give the use of the output to the paid tier only (checked 2026-11-22), and the maintainer's resource is the free tier (F0). The player plays this set instead when it is there.
  - `uv run python scripts/film_voice.py` makes the missing Google clips, `--provider azure` the local ones (the keys in `learner/secrets.env`; `--list` shows the texts).
- **The background** (`ambience/f1.mp3`, `f2.mp3`, `f3.mp3`, 2026-11-22): a quiet 30-second loop under the voice, one per film. f1 is a mountainside at Delphi (gusting wind with a thin whistle, goat bells far off with an echo), f2 a village by day (a light breeze, three cicadas, now and then a bell), f3 noon under the olive tree (warm air, leaves, a chorus of five cicadas that swells and fades).
  - **Original, CC BY-SA 4.0** like the rest of this folder: nothing is recorded or downloaded. `scripts/film_ambience.py` makes every sound from seeded noise and sine waves (filtered noise for the wind and leaves, a pulsing narrow band for each cicada, a few inharmonic partials for a bell), so the same command makes the same files. It needs only the standard library, and ffmpeg (libmp3lame) on the PATH to encode: `uv run python scripts/film_ambience.py` remakes all three in about 20 seconds (`f3` for one, `--wav <folder>` keeps the WAVs to listen to). 22.05 kHz stereo MP3 at 64 kbps, about 235 KB each.
  - The loop is seamless: the slow changes (gusts, swells, the cicadas' pulse) repeat a whole number of times in it, the noise is cross-faded from its end into its start, and a bell near the end rings on at the start.
  - It plays only with **▶ Play with sound**: it fades in, sinks under each clip (from a moment before the clip, quicker down than up), fades out over the last seconds, on a pause and when the page is hidden, and **Background off / Arka sesi kapat** turns it off (remembered in the browser). Its levels are `BED` and `DUCK` in `film.js`: the loops are made at about −21 to −19 LUFS, so with the voice clips at about −17 LUFS the background sits 15–17 dB below the voice between the lines and 20–23 dB under them (lowered once at the maintainer's ask, 2026-11-22). Served, it loops through Web Audio (no gap); opened as a file, where a page may not fetch, through a looping `<audio>`.

The source text: Pausanias, *Description of Greece* 10.29.1–2. The captions are new Greek written for the app, not quotations.
