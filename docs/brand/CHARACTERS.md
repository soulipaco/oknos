# The story's people: the character sheet

The open book (`content/open/open-book/`) tells one story: Zeynep, from Izmir, moves to Thessaloniki for work (`lessons/outline.json`, `cast`). Its scene pictures show the same people again and again, so each recurring person has one fixed look, written here and pasted word for word into every prompt that shows them. The figures follow the People rules in `docs/brand/IMAGES.md` (dot eyes, a small mouth line, no other facial detail): they are characters, never portraits of anyone, and a prompt never names a real or famous person.

## The look strings

Each line below is pasted into a prompt as it stands. Change a look only here, and then make the reference picture again.

### Ζεϊνέπ (Zeynep): the learner of the story

> Zeynep: a woman of about 30, short and athletic, light skin, a sleek straight jet-black bob cut level with her chin, parted on the side; a smart outfit of a black blazer over a white top, a red belt and blue tailored trousers, black flat shoes; she often carries a red tote bag with a book in it.

- At work (the pharmacy): the same hair and trousers, with a white pharmacist's coat instead of the blazer.
- At the sea or doing sport: the same hair, a blue sports top or a red swimsuit.
- What she is like: she reads a lot, does sport and loves the sea; small touches in a scene can show it (a book in her bag, a towel, trainers).
- Colours: black is drawn in the ink colour `#1e2a32`, red in terracotta `#b0503a`, blue in Aegean `#1f5f8b`.

### Κατερίνα (κυρία Κατερίνα): the landlady and neighbour

> Katerina: a woman of about 65, a little plump, warm beige skin, silver-grey hair in a low bun, small round glasses; an olive-green cardigan over a light terracotta dress, comfortable brown shoes.

### Γιώργος (Giorgos): Katerina's son, Zeynep's friend

> Giorgos: a man of about 33, tall, warm olive skin, short curly dark-brown hair and a short dark beard; a light blue shirt with the sleeves rolled up, dark blue jeans, white trainers.

### People who appear once or twice

Short looks, kept the same wherever they come back:

- **Σοφία** (Sofia), the colleague at the pharmacy: a woman of about 40, brown skin, long dark curly hair in a ponytail, a white pharmacist's coat.
- **κύριος Παύλος** (Pavlos), the evening course's teacher: a man of about 55, light skin, grey hair and a grey moustache, a terracotta jumper over a white collar.
- **Μάρκο** (Marco), the classmate from Italy: a man of about 25, light olive skin, wavy brown hair, an olive-green hoodie.
- **Ελένη** (Eleni), the baker: a woman of about 50, warm beige skin, brown hair under a white cap, a white apron.
- **Στέλιος** (Stelios), the greengrocer at the street market: a man of about 60, tanned skin, bald with a white moustache, a green apron over a blue shirt.

## Reference pictures

The recurring three have a reference sheet (the same person three times, same outfit) in `docs/brand/characters/`. A scene prompt names the person with the look string and, where the image tool takes a picture as input, the reference sheet too.

## Scenes

A scene is one picture for a moment of a chapter (Zeynep at the bakery, at the street market). It follows `docs/brand/IMAGES.md` with three changes: up to three people, a simple cropped setting instead of a plain background, and things in it the chapter names (bread, tomatoes, a scale), so the exercises can ask about them. Still no text, numbers, signs or logos anywhere: prices and hours stay in the chapter's Greek.
